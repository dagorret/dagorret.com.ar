Future population depends on fertility, survival, and age structure. This Lab varies expected fertility relative to a reference path and shows how a logistic curve changes through 2100.

## UN reference, Lab model

The UN’s WPP 2024 summary gives roughly 8.2 billion people in 2024 and 10.2 billion in 2100 for its medium reference, following a peak in the 2080s. Low and high variants shift fertility by approximately half a birth per woman relative to the medium trajectory. These are assumption-based scenarios, not confidence intervals.

The slider represents that **fertility shift**, not a constant global fertility rate. The plotted curves are our own teaching model: a simple logistic cannot reproduce the peak and subsequent decline of the official projection.

## What the slider recalculates

The model trajectory is:

$$
P(t)=\frac{K}{1+(K/P_0-1)e^{-r(t-2024)}}.
$$

We fix $P_0=8.2$ and $r=0.035$. The central plateau $K_0$ is calibrated to reach $P(2100)=10.2$. With these values, the model’s implied initial growth rate, $r(1-P_0/K_0)$, is about 0.7% a year in the medium scenario, below the roughly 0.9% growth the UN observed for 2024: the logistic gives up that initial fit in order to reach the 2100 value. The control $\Delta f$ changes that plateau using an illustrative sensitivity chosen for this experiment:

$$
K(\Delta f)=K_0\,e^{0.75\Delta f},\qquad -0.5\leq\Delta f\leq0.5.
$$

The coefficient 0.75 is not a UN estimate. The plateau is the model’s mathematical asymptote, not an ecological limit or validated forecast. In the low scenario it can fall below the initial value and cause immediate decline: this model omits age-structure momentum. Explore that mechanism in the [fertility and replacement Lab](/en/lab/birth-rate/).

**Sources:** [UN, WPP 2024 summary](https://www.un.org/en/UN-projects-world-population-to-peak-within-this-century) and [projection methodology](https://www.un.org/development/desa/pd/sites/www.un.org.development.desa.pd/files/files/documents/2024/Jul/undesa_pd_2024_wpp2024_methodology-report_web.pdf).
